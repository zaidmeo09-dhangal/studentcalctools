**Sign in to your PowerSchool portal and look in Grades and Attendance and Grades History for a GPA or class rank field. Some schools show GPA, a few also show class rank, and many show neither, so if you can't find your rank, ask your school counselor.**

That is the honest answer, and here is why. PowerSchool is the same software in thousands of schools, but each school decides what students and parents can see. PowerSchool's [parent and student resource center](https://www.powerschool.com/community-support/parent-student-resource-center/) says features such as GPA are configured school by school by your district's PowerSchool administrators. This guide shows the real menu names, where GPA appears on the web and in the app, why class rank is so often missing, and what to do about it. If you already know your GPA and class size and just want a number, you can [estimate your class rank](https://studentcalctools.com/class-rank-calculator/) in a minute.



## **Key takeaways**

- PowerSchool does not show the same screens to every student. Your school's administrators control what appears.
- The two places to check first on the web are **Grades and Attendance** and **Grades History**.
- In the mobile app, GPA is one of the dashboard widgets. Class rank is not on PowerSchool's list of dashboard widgets.
- Class rank is calculated separately from GPA, often on a schedule, and some schools only show it on a transcript or not at all.
- If your rank is missing, your counselor can give you the number, the class size and the date it was last updated.



## **Can you see your class rank on PowerSchool?**

Sometimes. PowerSchool's admin help says administrators can [control the data that students and parents see](https://ps.powerschool-docs.com/pssis-admin/latest/display-options) in the portal. Its student and parent help adds that the navigation menu [may include extra links your school has turned on](https://ps.powerschool-docs.com/pssis-student-parent/latest/more).

Here is what the official help and PowerSchool's own app listing say about each screen:


| **Screen** | **What PowerSchool says it shows** | **GPA or class rank?** |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Grades and Attendance](https://ps.powerschool-docs.com/pssis-student-parent/latest/grades-and-attendance) (web) | Current term grades, attendance and assignment scores | A current GPA can appear at the bottom of this page if your school turns it on, according to a [PowerSchool users group guide](https://www.psugcal.org/images/5/59/ParentandStudentPortalAvailabilitySettingsv1.pdf) |
| [Grades History](https://ps.powerschool-docs.com/pssis-student-parent/latest/grades-history) (web) | Term grades with the course, letter grade, percentage, citizenship grade and credit hours | Not listed on the help page |
| [Graduation Progress](https://ps.powerschool-docs.com/pssis-student-parent/latest/graduation-progress) (web) | Credits earned and still needed for your graduation plan | Credits, not rank |
| [PowerSchool Mobile dashboard](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/powerschool-mobile/id973741088) (app) | Widgets for GPA, class overview, assignments due and graded, attendance, bulletins, meal balance and fees | GPA is listed. Class rank is not |
| Report card or transcript (if your school adds a link) | Varies by school | Some districts show cumulative GPA only here |


None of PowerSchool's student and parent help pages for Grades and Attendance, Grades History or Graduation Progress mention class rank. Class rank does exist in PowerSchool, but as something schools calculate and then choose where to show.

## **How to find your GPA and class rank in the PowerSchool web portal**

1. **Open your school's PowerSchool portal address.** You need your school's web address, a username and a password. If you don't have them, [PowerSchool's help says to contact your school](https://ps.powerschool-docs.com/pssis-admin/latest/work-with-powerschool-student-and-parent-portal). Never use someone else's login or share yours.
2. **Sign in with the right account.** If the page has separate sections for students and parents, use the one that matches your account.
3. **Select Grades and Attendance on the start page.** This page shows your current term grades. Scroll to the bottom and check for a current GPA line.
4. **Click Grades History in the navigation menu.** Use the term tabs to switch between terms, and look for GPA or class rank labels next to your course list.
5. **Scan the rest of the menu.** Some schools add links such as report cards or transcripts, where cumulative GPA or rank may appear.
6. **Read the label before you trust the number.** A "current GPA" and a "cumulative GPA" are different things (see the next section).
7. **Still nothing? Use View Counselors.** On the Grades History page, the View Counselors button shows who your counselor is. That is the person to ask.

## **How to find your GPA and class rank in the PowerSchool mobile app**

1. **Set up the app.** PowerSchool Mobile only works if your district uses PowerSchool's student information system. You sign in with your portal username and password and enter your school's district code. Your school can give you the code, and in some portals it shows on the web page after you sign in ([example guide](https://cdnsm5-ss19.sharpschool.com/UserFiles/Servers/Server_26883569/File/Parents/Parent%20Portal/Guide%20to%20PowerSchool%20Parent%20Portal%20-%20Updated%202025.pdf)).
2. **Stay on the Dashboard.** It is the home screen. PowerSchool says the dashboard has widgets for GPA, class overview, assignments due, assignments graded, attendance, school bulletins, meal balance and fees.
3. **Look for the GPA widget.** The app also lets you reorder or hide widgets, so check that you have not hidden it.
4. **Open your classes for grades by term.** The app lets you view class information and grades across terms and open details for assignments, attendance and standards.
5. **Don't expect a class rank widget.** Class rank is not on PowerSchool's widget list for the app.

Menu names change between app versions. PowerSchool's current product page lists Dashboards, Classes, Calendar, Account and Forms, while older district guides mention Schedule and More tabs. PowerSchool's release notes for version 26.8 also say features a district does not use should no longer appear, so a missing GPA widget can mean your school has not turned GPA on.

## **What does the GPA on PowerSchool mean? Weighted, simple and current**

Your school chooses which GPA PowerSchool displays, so the label matters. According to PowerSchool's [GPA Student Screens help](https://ps.powerschool-docs.com/pssis-admin/latest/gpa-student-screens), a school can show these types:


| **Label** | **Plain meaning** |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Weighted | Includes the credit hours each course earns in the GPA calculation |
| Simple | Uses only your grades. Credit hours are not part of the calculation |
| Weighted percent or simple percent | An average percentage instead of a 4.0 grade point number |
| Current GPA | Based on your in-progress grades, and PowerSchool says current grades are always calculated with the simple method |
| Cumulative GPA | Built from stored grades across your completed terms. Your school chooses which cumulative rows to show |


Two things trip students up here:

- **"Weighted" in PowerSchool does not automatically mean honors or AP bonus points.** In PowerSchool's wording it refers to credit hours. Whether honors or AP classes add points depends on your school's own GPA rules, so ask your counselor.
- **Stored grades matter.** PowerSchool says administrators copy current grades into historical grades at the end of each grading term, and [historical grades appear on report cards and transcripts](https://ps.powerschool-docs.com/pssis-admin/latest/historical-grades). That is why a cumulative GPA can look out of date until a term is closed.

If you want to double-check the number, read our guide on [how to calculate GPA](https://studentcalctools.com/blog/how-to-calculate-gpa/) or [calculate your GPA](https://studentcalctools.com/gpa-calculator/) from your grades and credits. Our calculator uses the standard 4.0 scale and credit hours, so it will not match a school that adds honors or AP points.



## **Why can't you see your class rank on PowerSchool?**

There is usually a plain reason. These are the most common ones, based on PowerSchool's own documentation:


| **Reason** | **What it means for you** |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Your school has not turned the display on | Administrators control what students and parents see, so GPA and other items can be switched off school by school |
| Your school shows rank only on a transcript or report card | Some districts keep cumulative GPA on transcripts only. [Levittown Public Schools](https://www.levittownschools.com/parentportalnewsletter/) says this in its parent portal FAQ |
| Rank is calculated on a schedule | [PowerSchool says class rank is calculated](https://ps.powerschool-docs.com/pssis-admin/latest/class-rank) manually or automatically at set intervals, such as every week or only after grades are stored. It is not live |
| You may be outside the ranking pool | PowerSchool lets schools exclude students who are flagged as excluded from rank, such as students enrolled for a short time, and count only courses that count toward rank. Your rank can differ from your GPA for that reason |
| Your GPA is not calculated yet | PowerSchool bases class rank on GPA, so a rank cannot exist until the GPA calculation is set up |
| Your school does not rank at all | Some schools do not report class rank, or only report a percentile or a top-percent band |

## **What to do if PowerSchool does not show your rank**

Don't guess. Get the number from the school, and use an estimate only as a stand-in until you do.

1. **Ask your counselor.** Use the View Counselors button on Grades History to find the right person.
2. **Ask for an unofficial transcript.** Many schools print rank or class standing on the transcript.
3. **Ask four specific questions:** What is my current rank and class size? Is it weighted or unweighted? When was it last updated? Does the school report a number, a percentile or a top-percent band?
4. **Estimate it while you wait.** If you know your rank and class size, or your GPA compared with your class, our [class rank calculator](https://studentcalctools.com/class-rank-calculator/) gives an estimate. It is not an official rank.

Here is a short message you can send:

Hi [Counselor name], I'm a [grade] student and I couldn't find my class rank in PowerSchool. Could you tell me my current rank, the class size, whether it is weighted or unweighted, and the date it was last updated? Thank you.

The date matters because PowerSchool has a field for the date a rank was last updated, so your counselor can usually see how current the number is.



## **Final thoughts**

PowerSchool can show your GPA, and sometimes your class rank, but your school makes the final call on what you see. Check Grades and Attendance and Grades History on the web, check the GPA widget in the app, and read the label on any number you find.
