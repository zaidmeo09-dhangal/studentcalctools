  Where is class rank on Skyward?

  On older screens, click Gradebook and then the GPA/Class Rank tab. On newer
  screens, click the Grading tile and open the Rank tab. A transcript in the
  Portfolio tile may also list rank.

  Why is my rank blank on Skyward?

  Common causes are that your school has not run the rank calculation, does not
  publish rank to families or students, or lets seniors opt out of showing it.
  Ask your counselor which one applies.

  ---

  What does Date Run mean on the Rank tab?

  It is the date that rank record was calculated. Rank records are listed with
  the most recent Date Run first, so the top row is the newest.

  Does the Skyward app show class rank?

  Skyward's app listing mentions grades, attendance and discipline, and it does
  not name GPA or rank. If you can't find them in the app, sign in through a web

  your counselor if something is missing.

**Sign in to Skyward Family Access or Student Access, then open the Gradebook (older screens) or the Grading tile (newer screens) and look for a GPA/Class Rank tab, or separate GPA and Rank tabs. If rank is not there, your school has not published it to you, so check your transcript in Portfolio or ask your counselor.**

Skyward has built-in places for both GPA and class rank, and Skyward's own [Family Access guide](https://www.skyward.com/support/parents-and-students/family-access-toolkit/parents/power-up-grades) says information about GPA and class rank can be found in the gradebook. The catch is that your school decides what it publishes, and Skyward comes in two versions with different menus. This guide covers both, with the real tab names from Skyward and district help pages. If you already know your GPA and class size and want a quick number, you can [estimate your class rank](https://studentcalctools.com/class-rank-calculator/) in a minute. If your school uses PowerSchool instead, read our guide on [class rank on PowerSchool](https://studentcalctools.com/blog/class-rank-gpa-powerschool/).



## **Key takeaways**

- Skyward can show GPA and class rank, but each school or district chooses what students and parents see.
- On older Skyward screens, look for the **GPA/Class Rank** tab inside the Gradebook.
- On newer screens, open the **Grading** tile and use the **GPA** and **Rank** tabs.
- Your transcript in the **Portfolio** tile may list rank and class size even when the tabs are empty.
- If rank is blank, the school may not have run it yet, may not publish it, or may let seniors opt out. Your counselor can tell you which.



## **Can you see your class rank on Skyward?**

Often, yes. Here is what Skyward and district help pages say about each area of Family Access and Student Access:


|  |  |  |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Skyward area** | **What it shows** | **GPA or class rank?** |
| Gradebook (older screens) | Grades by term, with Display Options | A GPA/Class Rank tab, according to [Garland ISD's Skyward guide](https://www.garlandisdschools.net/uploaded/high_schools/sghs/documents/Skyward_Help_-_Gradebook_Information.pdf) |
| Grading tile (newer screens) | Grades, GPA and Rank tabs | Yes, per [Pasadena ISD](https://sites.google.com/pasadenaisd.org/skywardfamilyaccess/home/features-tiles/grading) and [La Porte ISD](https://lpisd.org/236198_2) guides |
| Academic History | Courses and grades from previous years | Not described as a rank screen |
| Portfolio | Report cards, progress reports and transcripts | A transcript can show rank and class size, and Garland ISD lists a GPA report here too |
| Skyward Mobile Access app | Grades, attendance, calendar, fees and messages | Skyward's listing does not name GPA or rank |


The reason the answer is "it depends" is that rank is something your school sets up and runs. Skyward's own training catalog describes configuring GPA "for later use on things like honor roll and rank" as part of grading setup ([Skyward Qmlativ training catalog](https://www.skyward.com/web/media/PDFs/Qmlativ-PDC-March2023.pdf)). And students may not see everything parents can: [Garland ISD](https://www.garlandisd.net/programs-services/skyward-help) warns that student accounts may not have access to all of the same information as parent accounts.



## **Which version of Skyward do you have?**

Skyward currently supports two products. Its own site lists a support center for **SMS 2.0**, the older system, and a separate area for **Qmlativ**, the newer one. Districts are moving over time. Lewisville ISD, for example, [announced it would use Skyward Qmlativ starting in the 2026-2027 school year](https://khub.lisd.net/authors/1152965).

Here is a simple way to tell them apart, based on how district guides describe the screens:

- **Older screens:** a **Gradebook** button or tab, then a **GPA/Class Rank** tab.
- **Newer screens:** tiles on the home page, such as **Grading** and **Portfolio**, and inside Grading, tabs for **Grades**, **GPA** and **Rank**.

If you can't tell, try both sets of steps below. Your school's Skyward help page will say which one it uses.

## **How to find GPA and class rank on Skyward (newer screens)**

1. **Sign in to your district's Skyward page.** If you don't know the address, Skyward's [login help page](https://www.skyward.com/login) or your school website will point you to it. Students use their own account, and Pasadena ISD says students sign in with their district login.
2. **Pick the right portal.** If you have access to more than one, [click the down arrow next to the home icon and choose Family Access](https://khub.lisd.net/gpa-history-transcripts/viewing-transcripts-in-the-student-portfolio) (or the portal that matches your account).
3. **Choose the student.** If you are a parent with more than one child, select the child's name at the top left or use a tile under that child's name.
4. **Click the Grading tile.**
5. **Choose Current Year or All Years.** Radio buttons near the top of the Student Grades screen switch between the current school year and every year the student has attended.
6. **Open the GPA tab.** GPA records show Cumulative buckets first, then by school year and GPA bucket. The columns are School Year, GPA Bucket, GPA Method, Earned Credits, GPA Points and GPA.
7. **Open the Rank tab.** Rank records show the Rank Method, Rank, Grading Term and Date Run, with the most recent Date Run first.
8. **No Rank tab, or no rows?** Go to the section on why rank may be blank.

![Generic grading screen mockup with Grades, GPA and Rank tabs and a Current Year toggle](/assets/images/academic-grading-dashboard-interface.webp)

## **How to find GPA and class rank on Skyward (older screens)**

1. **Log in to Family Access or Student Access.**
2. **Select one student.** In [Garland ISD's Skyward guide](https://garlandisd.net/programs-services/skyward-help/skyward-gradebook), the Gradebook will not open if "All Students" is selected, so pick a specific student first.
3. **Click Gradebook.** Skyward's own guide says this is where you see your classes and grades by term, and you can change the term under Display Options.
4. **Click the GPA/Class Rank tab.** A pop-up window shows the GPA and class rank. An older district guide for Student Access also lists this tab as [GPA/Class Rank (high school only)](https://core-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/3405/AHS/2977338/Skyward_Student_Access_Instructions.pdf), with a View Details option that shows the GPA type.

## **How to check GPA and rank in the Skyward mobile app**

1. **Download Skyward Mobile Access.** It is free in the App Store and Google Play. According to [Skyward's App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id502635374), your district must activate Mobile Access Automatic Setup for the app to find your school.
2. **Find your district.** Skyward's setup video, quoted in [Lewisville ISD's app guide](https://khub.lisd.net/sis-navigation/skyward-mobile-app), says you can use your current location, search by zip code or type the district name.
3. **Choose the right product.** Select Student Management if you are a parent or student using Family Access.
4. **Sign in and set a passcode.** Enter your Skyward credentials and create the four-digit passcode.
5. **Use the home page tiles.** Tap a tile to jump to that page, or open the menu with the three lines next to the back arrow.
6. **Look for GPA or Rank.** Skyward's listing says the app gives access to grades, attendance, discipline and more, and it does not name GPA or class rank. If you can't find them, sign in through a web browser instead.

Not seeing GPA or rank in the app is normal. Lewisville ISD even recommends a computer, or the **Open in Browser** option when logging into the app, to get all the features of some pages, such as transcripts.



## **Where does GPA show up, and what do the labels mean?**

Skyward shows GPA as a set of records, and the names can look technical. In plain English:


|  |  |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Label** | **What it usually means** |
| GPA Bucket | One named GPA your school tracks, such as a cumulative GPA or a GPA for one school year |
| GPA Method | The calculation your school uses for that GPA |
| Earned Credits and GPA Points | The credits and grade points that feed the GPA |
| Cumulative | Built from all completed courses so far. These records appear first |
| Current Year or All Years | The filter at the top, not a type of GPA |


Weighted and unweighted GPAs are defined by your school, not by Skyward. Lewisville ISD's transcript shows the difference clearly. Its [cumulative unweighted GPA](https://khub.lisd.net/gpa-history-transcripts/viewing-transcripts-in-the-student-portfolio) looks at all state courses on a 4.0 scale without weights, while its cumulative weighted GPA only counts core subjects and weights advanced courses such as Honors and AP higher. Your school may use different rules, so read the label and ask.

If you want to double-check a number, you can [calculate your GPA](https://studentcalctools.com/gpa-calculator/) from your grades and credits. Our calculator uses the standard 4.0 scale and credit hours and does not add honors or AP points, so it will not match a school that does.



## **Why is your class rank blank on Skyward?**

There is usually a plain reason:


|  |  |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reason** | **What it means for you** |
| Rank has not been run | Rank records have a Date Run, and Skyward staff run the ranking process as part of grading. No run means no rank yet. [This tutorial for staff](https://www.iorad.com/player/166495/Skyward---Calculate-GPA) shows the Run Grad Req Rank GPA step |
| Your school does not publish it | Rank is set up by the school, and some districts only show it on a transcript or report |
| A senior opted out | In Lewisville ISD, rank appears on the transcript unless the student is a senior who [opted out](https://khub.lisd.net/gpa-history-transcripts/student-rank-display-on-transcript-opt-out-process) of displaying it |
| Student and parent accounts differ | Students may not see everything a parent account can |
| Wrong filter | Check Current Year versus All Years, and make sure one student is selected, not All Students |
| Your school does not rank | Some schools do not report class rank at all, or only report a percentile or a top-percent band |


For more on what the number means once you have it, see [where to find your class rank](https://studentcalctools.com/blog/what-is-my-class-rank/).

## **What to do if Skyward does not show your rank**

1. **Open the Portfolio tile and look for a transcript.** In Lewisville ISD, transcripts are attachments in Portfolio. Click the eyeball icon for the current-year transcript, or the download icon at any time with fewer features. A [Comal ISD announcement](https://www.comalisd.org/apps/news/article/1458085) says its transcripts list Class Rank, Class Size, GPA and Quartile Rank in the bottom left corner.
2. **Ask your counselor.** Request your current rank, the class size, whether the rank is weighted or unweighted, and the date it was last calculated.
3. **Estimate it while you wait.** If you know your GPA and rank or class size, our [class rank calculator](https://studentcalctools.com/class-rank-calculator/) gives an estimate. It is not an official rank.

Here is a short message you can send:

Hi [Counselor name], I'm a [grade] student and I couldn't find my class rank in Skyward. Could you tell me my current rank, the class size, whether it is weighted or unweighted, and the date it was last calculated? Thank you.



## **Final thoughts**

Skyward can show your GPA and class rank, but your school controls what you see and when rank is calculated. Check the Gradebook or the Grading tile first, then your transcript in Portfolio.
