THE 4.33 SCALE

Standard GPA vs. LSAC (CAS) GPA

The biggest difference between your standard college GPA and the LSAC GPA is the treatment of the A+ grade. Most undergraduate institutions cap their GPA at 4.0. The Law School Admission Council (LSAC) rewards an A+ with a 4.33. This means students with A+ grades on their transcript can actually achieve an LSAC GPA higher than a 4.0.

Retakes, Withdrawals, and Master's Degrees

!

Retaken Courses

Unlike standard grade forgiveness, LSAC counts ALL attempts of a class. You must enter both the original fail and the retaken pass.

!

Withdrawals (W vs WF)

A standard "W" (Withdraw) is excluded. However, a punitive "WF" (Withdraw Fail) is counted by LSAC as a 0.0, severely impacting your GPA.

!

Graduate Degrees

LSAC only calculates grades earned prior to your first bachelor's degree. Do not enter Master's or Ph.D. grades here.
