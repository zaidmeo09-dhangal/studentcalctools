About Ethan Cole

Ethan Cole is the editorial pen name behind StudentCalcTools. He writes and reviews practical guides about GPA, grades, attendance, class rank, study planning and education costs.

Through StudentCalcTools, Ethan has reviewed 10 browser-based student calculators. His review process checks formulas, input handling, rounding, examples, assumptions and stated limitations. Important academic and financial guidance is cross-checked with official sources where available.
