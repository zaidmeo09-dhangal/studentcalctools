HOW IT WORKS

How Attendance is Calculated

Attendance percentage is the number of classes you attended divided by the total number of classes held, multiplied by 100. The formula is:

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes Held) × 100

For example, if your college has held 45 lectures and you attended 38 of them, your attendance is (38 ÷ 45) × 100 = 84.4%. Most colleges and universities require a minimum of 75% attendance. Some courses are stricter and require 85% or higher.

This calculator also works in reverse: enter your target percentage and remaining classes, and it tells you exactly how many lectures you can still skip — or how many you must attend — to hit your goal.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How is attendance percentage calculated?+
Attendance percentage is calculated by dividing the number of classes attended by the total number of classes held, multiplied by 100.
What is a safe attendance percentage?+
Most colleges and schools require a minimum of 75 percent attendance. Maintaining 80-85% provides a safety buffer in case you miss classes.
How many classes can I miss?+
This calculator tells you exactly how many more classes you can miss while staying above your target attendance percentage.
Is my data saved?+
No. All calculations happen in your browser. Nothing is stored or shared.
What happens if my attendance falls below 75%?+
Policies vary by institution. Common consequences include being barred from exams, academic probation, or immediate failure of the course.
Is attendance calculated per subject or overall?+
Most colleges track attendance separately for each subject, so you must maintain the required percentage in each individual course, not just overall.
